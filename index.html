Why a macro pad should feel like a wave.

Traditional macro pads are grids of buttons. SurfBoard is a living surface — a touchscreen that can become whatever your workflow needs: control room, stream deck, synth panel, or status board.

Fluid by design

The square capacitive display lets layouts shift, morph, and adapt. Scenes, pages, and modes can flow instead of being locked to fixed keys.

Built on Raspberry Pi

Each SurfBoard runs on a Raspberry Pi, giving you a familiar, hackable platform with a massive ecosystem of tools and libraries.

For people who build

From Lite to Maker Edition, every model is designed for people who tinker, script, automate, and ship things into the world.
