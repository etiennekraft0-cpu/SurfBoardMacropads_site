Small footprint, big impact.

SurfBoard Lite focuses on the essentials: a responsive square display, a capable Raspberry Pi, and a layout that feels instantly understandable. It’s the macro pad you actually want to keep on your desk.

Perfect starter board

Lite is ideal if you’re new to macro pads or just want a clean, focused surface for a few key workflows — streaming controls, media, shortcuts, or a tiny status dashboard.

Powered by Pi Zero 2 W

The Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W gives Lite enough power to run modern UIs, dashboards, and automation scripts without overcomplicating the hardware.

Designed to grow with you

Optional GPIO access means you can start simple and later experiment with sensors, LEDs, or external controls as your curiosity grows.
